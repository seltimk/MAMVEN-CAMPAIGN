Meet The Candidate

Who is Apostle Jack Light Mamven?

A dedicated servant leader, faith minister, community advocate, and champion for good governance in Langtang North & Langtang South.

Life & Legacy

A Life Dedicated to Faith, Family & Public Service

Apostle Jack Light Mamven was born and raised in the heart of Plateau State. From his early years, he possessed an unshakeable belief that genuine leadership is defined by the depth of service to one's community.

Over the past two decades, Apostle Mamven has served as a minister of faith, a grassroots community organizer, and a defender of human rights. His landmark work in anti-human trafficking has brought hope and freedom to hundreds of vulnerable young people across Nigeria.

Standing under the banner of the Allied Peoples Movement (APM), Apostle Mamven is running for Member, House of Representatives (Langtang North & Langtang South Federal Constituency) to bring transparent, accountable, and result-oriented governance to the National Assembly.

Key Qualifications & Standing:
  • Candidate for Member, House of Representatives
  • Langtang North & Langtang South Federal Constituency
  • Political Party: APM (Allied Peoples Movement)
  • Founder & Patron, Community Anti-Trafficking Shield Initiative
Stand With Apostle Jack

Our Mission

To represent the people of Langtang North & South with absolute integrity in the House of Representatives, ensuring federal resources are equitably deployed for education, modern agriculture, youth employment, and robust security for every ward.

Our Vision

A prosperous, peaceful, and self-sufficient Langtang Federal Constituency where every child is educated, every farmer thrives, every woman is empowered, and human trafficking is completely eradicated.

Guiding Principles

Our Seven Core Values

The moral and strategic foundation behind every decision we make.

1. Hope

Restoring faith in public service through practical results and accessible governance.

2. Integrity

Uncompromising moral uprightness in managing public trust and constituency funds.

3. Servant Leadership

Putting the welfare of our people first before any personal or political interest.

4. Community First

Grassroots inclusion ensuring every ward in Langtang North and South has a voice.

5. Transparency

Open-door policy with quarterly constituency town halls and published audit summaries.

6. Accessibility

Maintaining active constituency offices easily reachable by every citizen.
