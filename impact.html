Action Plan

Legislative Impact & Development Pillars

Delivering targeted federal intervention and grassroots empowerment across 7 vital sectors in Langtang North & Langtang South.

Sector 01

Community Development

Establishing ward-level dialogue committees to ensure local priorities directly shape federal constituency projects, solar street lighting, and civic halls.

Learn More
Sector 02

Quality Education

Annual scholarship funds for higher education students, rehabilitation of public primary schools, and free JAMB/WAEC registration assistance.

Learn More
Sector 03

Youth Empowerment

Digital tech hubs, artisan vocational centers, and entrepreneurship start-up grants to turn unemployed youth into self-reliant leaders.

Learn More
Sector 04

Women Support & Micro-Grants

Revolving zero-interest micro-credit schemes for market women, maternal healthcare welfare kits, and leadership mentorship programs.

Learn More
Sector 05

Agriculture & Food Security

Provision of high-yield seeds, subsidized fertilizer, tractors, and solar-powered irrigation wells to boost yam, cassava, and grain production.

Learn More
Sector 06

Healthcare

Medical outreach programs, upgrade of rural primary healthcare centers, and essential medicine distribution.

Learn More
Sector 07 • Flagship Priority

Rural Infrastructure & Clean Water Expansion

Securing federal budgetary allocations for grading rural feeder roads connecting Langtang North & South farming communities to urban markets, and drilling 50+ motorized solar boreholes.
