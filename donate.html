Transparency Guarantee

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about contributing to our campaign fund.

Who can donate to this campaign?

Any citizen of Nigeria, indigene of Langtang North & South, or corporate supporter committed to democratic progress and good governance can contribute within INEC electoral guidelines.

Is my financial donation tax-deductible or audited?

Yes! All campaign contributions are tracked by our official campaign finance compliance team, and quarterly audit summaries are published for maximum transparency.

Can I donate equipment or items instead of cash?

Absolutely. We welcome in-kind donations such as campaign buses, public address systems, printing services, and medical supplies for our outreach clinics.
