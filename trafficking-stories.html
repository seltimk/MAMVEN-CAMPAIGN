Reclaiming Dreams After 2 Years of Captivity

Lured away under false promises of university education abroad, Blessing was stranded in a human trafficking syndicate. Through the intervention of Apostle Jack Light Mamven's legal team and security intelligence, Blessing was safely repatriated and enrolled in a full vocational tech scholarship program.
